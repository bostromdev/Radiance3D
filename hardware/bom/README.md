# Bill of materials

No tested BOM exists. A future BOM must distinguish required and optional parts,
identify exact manufacturer part numbers or functional requirements, record tested
revisions, and avoid implying compatibility for untested substitutions.

Physical dimensions for BOM entries belong in
[`Measurements/`](../../Measurements/README.md), not in the BOM itself. The BOM should
carry identification, quantity and function, and link to the measurement file for
geometry. The current BOM narrative is in
[`docs/hardware/bill-of-materials.md`](../../docs/hardware/bill-of-materials.md).

Measuring a part is not qualifying it. A measured dimension says nothing about whether
the part is thermally, electrically or mechanically suitable.
