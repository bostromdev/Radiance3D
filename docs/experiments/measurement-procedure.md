# Measurement procedure

No physical procedure is validated. A future scan record should document:

1. legal and safety review;
2. equipment revisions, warm-up, and configuration;
3. AUT orientation and coordinate reference;
4. geometry, surroundings, feedline routing, and frequency;
5. zeroing, limits, angular sequence, dwell, and receiver settings;
6. reference measurements and environmental observations;
7. raw capture with timestamps, units, warnings, and provenance; and
8. shutdown plus a check for invalid or missing samples.
