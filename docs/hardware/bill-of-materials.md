# Bill of materials

There is no tested BOM. Candidate categories include an ESP32-class controller,
two stepper drivers, two appropriately sized motors, position/limit devices, power
conversion and protection, structure, bearings, fasteners, cabling, and RF equipment.

A publishable BOM must contain tested part numbers, revision, quantity, function,
acceptable substitutions, source date, and validation status. Price and availability
alone are not engineering validation.
