# AD8317 RF detector board

Physical measurements of the actual AD8317 detector board. These values override any
generic dimensions found online.

## Identification

- Component: AD8317 logarithmic RF power detector evaluation board
- Board silkscreen: **`AD8317 EVAL BD`** / **`NWDZ V1.0`** — legible in `IMG_5261.HEIC`
- Connectors: two edge-mount SMA, on opposite edges of the same axis. Silkscreen
  labels `RF1` (input) and `Vout` side. Header pads: `Tadj`, `Vadj`, `Vset`, `J2`,
  `+5V`, `GND`
- Quantity: 1
- Measurement source: photographs IMG_5257–5262
- Measured with: digital caliper, IP54, mm mode
- Units: mm

> [!NOTE]
> No RF detector is committed to yet. This board is documented because it was
> measured, not because it is selected.

## Derived from the measurements above

| Derived value | Result | Expression |
|---|---:|---|
| SMA protrusion per side | 9.78 mm | `(55.84 − 36.27) / 2` |
| Total SMA protrusion, both sides | 19.57 mm | `55.84 − 36.27` |

9.78 mm per connector is a normal edge-launch SMA protrusion, which corroborates that
36.27 mm and 55.84 mm are measuring the same axis.

## Resolved: the earlier axis-label conflict

An earlier report described these as "PCB height 36.27 mm (board only)" and "overall
height, SMA tip to SMA tip, 35.72 mm". As labelled that was impossible — a board cannot
be 0.55 mm taller *without* its connectors than with them.

The photographs settle it. **Both readings are bare-PCB dimensions on perpendicular
axes.** Neither includes an SMA connector:

- `IMG_5261.HEIC` — board with the SMAs left and right, jaws gripping the PCB edges
  *inside* the connectors. 36.27 mm, along the SMA axis.
- `IMG_5262.HEIC` — board rotated 90°, SMAs top and bottom, jaws on the PCB edges.
  35.72 mm, perpendicular to the SMA axis.

The 35.72 mm figure was never an SMA-tip-to-tip measurement. The only tip-to-tip
reading is 55.88 mm in `IMG_5257.HEIC`.

**The board is not square.** 36.27 × 35.72 mm is a real 0.55 mm difference between
axes, well outside this caliper's ~0.05 mm repeatability. Do not model it as square.

## CAD notes

- **Board outline:** 36.27 × 35.72 mm. Orient the model so the 36.27 mm axis is the
  one the SMAs leave from.
- **Important protrusions:** the two SMA connectors, 9.78 mm per side, giving a
  55.84 mm overall envelope along that axis. They need straight coax exits and must
  respect the coax bend radius, which is currently provisional.
- **Mounting-hole layout:** four plated corner holes are visible in `IMG_5261.HEIC`
  but neither diameter nor spacing has been measured. See
  [`missing-measurements.md`](missing-measurements.md).
- **Shield can:** 17.36 mm long, roughly centred on the board. Height above the PCB is
  not measured, so total board height is unknown.
- **Board thickness:** not measured.
- **Required print clearance:** any holder should be a removable fit, not an
  interference fit — this board carries RF and should not be stressed.
- **Unclear or missing dimensions:** see
  [`missing-measurements.md`](missing-measurements.md).
