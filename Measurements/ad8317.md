## Unresolved axis labelling

As labelled, the numbers are geometrically impossible:

```text
PCB height, board only, EXCLUDING connectors   36.27 mm
Overall height, INCLUDING both connectors      35.75 mm  (mean of 35.72 / 35.77)
```

A board cannot be 0.52 mm **taller** without its connectors than with them. One of the
two labels is wrong.

The most likely explanation is that **36.27 mm is the PCB *width*, not the height** —
the bare board across the axis the SMA connectors stick out of. That reading is
self-consistent:

- SMA pair adds `55.84 − 36.27` = 19.57 mm total, or **9.78 mm per connector**, which
  is a normal SMA protrusion.
- The bare PCB would then be **36.27 × 35.75 mm** — very nearly square, which is a
  common outline for an AD8317 breakout.

That is an inference, not a measurement, so it is **not** recorded as fact. Confirm it
before any of the height figures are used.

**To resolve:** one caliper reading of the bare PCB across each axis, with the
connectors excluded, plus a photograph of each. Two readings close it completely.

## CAD notes

Only two values are safe to use right now:

- **Overall width 55.84 mm** — both readings agree to 0.09 mm and the axis is
  unambiguous.
- **Shield-can length 17.34 mm** — unambiguous.

Everything on the height axis is blocked pending the clarification above.

- **Important protrusions:** the two SMA connectors dominate the outline, adding an
  estimated 9.78 mm per side. They need straight coax exits and must respect the coax
  bend radius.
- **Mounting-hole layout:** not measured.
- **Board thickness and total height:** not measured.
- **Status in Version 1:** no RF detector is committed to. See
  [`../docs/hardware/rf-measurement.md`](../docs/hardware/rf-measurement.md). This
  board is recorded because it was measured, not because it is selected.
- **Unclear or missing dimensions:** see
  [`missing-measurements.md`](missing-measurements.md).
